# Code of conduct

ARL contributors must keep discussion professional, evidence-led, and safe. Be
respectful across differences in experience, identity, language, and viewpoint. Give
specific technical feedback, distinguish facts from assumptions, preserve negative
results, and do not pressure others to disclose credentials or private data.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, credential
solicitation, deliberate disclosure of private vulnerability details, and attempts to
turn synthetic fault work into unauthorized real-system testing are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the repository's
private security/contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock
threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation. They should minimize access
to reports, avoid retaliation, document material enforcement decisions, and disclose
conflicts of interest.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
