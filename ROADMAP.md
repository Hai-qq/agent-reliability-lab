# Roadmap

Roadmap items are intentions, not completed capabilities or provider authorization.

## Near term

- Obtain independent review of `arl-evidence-v1`, paired estimands, and v0.30 power
  assumptions.
- Materialize v0.28/v0.29 episode evidence only if the original ignored inputs are
  available and pass a reviewed, read-only migration; otherwise retain
  `NOT_MATERIALIZED`.
- Complete branch, tag, Pages, and protected release-environment settings documented in
  the maintainer checklist.
- Archive the signed 0.4.0 release in a DOI-capable repository without inventing a DOI.

## Before any new provider study

- Freeze independent provider gateways, exact inexpensive model bindings, pricing,
  token budgets, source commit, v0.30 catalog, blocked schedule, and claim wording.
- Review the 48-template power simulation and preregister the final sample size.
- Run scripted preflight and a separately authorized bounded canary.
- Publish complete public evidence or declare exactly what cannot be materialized.

## Longer term

- Add reviewed third-party environment/fault/runtime adapters through lightweight entry
  points.
- Seek independent cross-provider and cross-implementation replication.
- Add new domains only with state-level evaluators, privacy review, and mutation suites.
